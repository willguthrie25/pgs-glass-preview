Overview

Safety and security glass

Toughened and laminated glass solve different problems and people mix them up constantly. Toughened is stronger and breaks into blunt fragments. Laminated is not necessarily stronger, but it stays in the frame when it breaks.

For security you almost always want laminated, because a toughened pane that has broken is an open hole.

Toughened glass

  • Heated to approximately 620°C then cooled fast
  • Four to five times the mechanical and thermal strength
  • 1(C)1 impact performance to EN 12600, Class A to BS 6206
  • Breaks into small fragments with dulled edges
  • Cannot be worked on once toughened
  • Softened surface is more prone to scratching

Laminated glass

  • Two or more sheets bonded with PVB interlayers
  • Impact performance of 1, 2 and 3 to EN 12600
  • Cuts ultraviolet radiation by up to 99%
  • Reduces sound transmission
  • Holds together when broken
  • Can be built up for manual attack, explosion and firearms
