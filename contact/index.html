Questions

Common questions

What do you need from me to price a job?
The postcode, what has broken or what you are specifying, and a photograph if you have one. That is usually enough to price it or to book a survey. Guessing from a description costs everyone a second visit.
Do you charge for a survey?
Quotations and surveys are free and carry no obligation.
Can you come out tonight?
We board up to make a property safe to make a property safe. Replacement glass is measured and fitted afterwards, because toughened and laminated units are made to size rather than carried on the van.
Can I visit a showroom?
There is no showroom. We work from two Kent workshops and we come to you, which is how glazing works when everything is made to the opening.
