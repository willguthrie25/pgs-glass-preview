Timber-framed fire screens and doors, rated E30 to EI90. The frame is FSC approved timber, which suits heritage and conservation work where a steel frame would be refused.

Overview

Timber fire screens and doors

Timber screen systems designed to sit in contemporary or traditional interiors, combined with tested timber door sets. The natural material, with the functionality of a fire protection system behind it.

Different glass combinations take the same frame from E30 through to EI90, so the appearance does not have to change when the rating does.

Questions

Common questions

Yes. The range covers internal and external applications, and external work is specified differently because the frame has to handle weather as well as fire.
The timber range covers E30 through to EI90. E is integrity only. EI adds insulation, which stops heat transferring through the glass as well as holding the fire back. Which one you need is set by the building, not by preference.
Appearance and context, usually. Timber suits heritage and conservation work where a steel frame would be refused, and it sits better in a domestic or hospitality interior. Steel takes longer spans and heavier traffic.
Integral blinds are an option, and the glass can be specified obscured where privacy matters. Both change the tested build-up, so they are decided before manufacture rather than after.
