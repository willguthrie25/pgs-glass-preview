Overview

Noise control glazing

Noise is a health problem before it is a comfort problem. Traffic, aircraft and neighbours cost people sleep and concentration, and glass is one of the few things that can be changed without moving.

Acoustic laminated glass uses a smart interlayer that stops sound frequencies vibrating from one pane through to the next. It keeps every safety property of ordinary laminated glass and comes in five standard thicknesses.

Options and applications

Secondary glazing benefits

  • Cuts noise by up to 80%, outperforming double glazing
  • Reduces heat loss, so lower fuel bills and less condensation
  • Cannot be opened from outside, so it adds security
  • Aluminium profile with a minimal sight line
