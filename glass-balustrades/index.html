Overview

Glass balustrades

A balustrade is a structural fixing that happens to be made of glass, which is why it gets surveyed rather than ordered off a description. Done properly it maximises light and space and disappears.

The post system uses stainless steel uprights and handrails, 304 grade inside and 316 outside, with the glass held by clamps. The frameless base channel and Spig-Lite systems give an uninterrupted view with no handrail line at all.

Process

How it runs

  1. 01

    Survey

    Measured on site, because a balustrade is a structural fixing and not a panel.

  2. 02

    Specification

    Glass thickness and system chosen against the load the balustrade has to take.

  3. 03

    Manufacture

    Toughened and laminated to the surveyed sizes.

  4. 04

    Installation

    Fixed, levelled and set.
