Specify

Rated to the drawing,
not to a guess

EW holds the fire back. EI holds it back and stops the heat coming through with it. Most people asking for 30 minute fire glass mean EW30, and most building control officers reading the drawing mean EI. That gap is the most common reason a screen gets rejected on site, and it is the first thing we check.

How it runs

Three steps, and we tell you where you are at each one

  1. Step 01

    Call

    Tell us the postcode and what has broken, or what you are specifying. That is usually enough to price it or book a survey.

  2. Step 02

    Survey

    We measure on site, or read the drawing and the building control requirement. Toughened glass cannot be cut once it is toughened, so the measure has to be right.

  3. Step 03

    Fit

    Made to the surveyed size, fitted to the tested detail rather than around it, and the mess taken away.
