Sealed units

Replacement sealed units, manufactured to BS EN 1279 and carrying a five year guarantee. When double glazing mists up it is the seal that has failed, not usually the window.

Overview

Sealed units

Choosing the right sealed unit is most of the job. The build-up decides the thermal performance, the acoustic performance and the safety class, and it is easier to get right before manufacture than after.

Integral blinds sit inside the unit, which means shading and privacy with nothing to clean. Five operating systems and a wide colour range.
