Overview

Decorative glass

Standard float glass has a green cast that is invisible until you put a colour behind it. Everything we back-paint uses Low Iron glass, so a white splashback reads white rather than faintly minty.

Splashbacks come in 4mm to 19mm, cut to shape and toughened. The standard colour range is wide and we can colour match almost anything outside it.
