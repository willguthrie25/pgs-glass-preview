Glazing repairs

Broken windows, blown double glazing, cracked shopfronts and smashed patio doors. We board up out of hours and replace the glass once it has been measured properly.

Overview

Glazing repairs

Our glaziers are experienced in every kind of glass repair, commercial and residential. The office is manned by trained staff, so a call gets an answer rather than a callback.

Misted double glazing is the one people expect to be expensive. It usually is not: the seal has failed, not the window, so we replace the sealed unit and leave the frame where it is at a fraction of the cost.

Process

How it runs

  1. 01

    Call

    Tell us what has broken and where. Postcode helps more than anything else.

  2. 02

    Make safe

    Boarding-up where the opening cannot be left as it is.

  3. 03

    Measure

    Proper measure once the opening is safe, because a guessed size fits nothing.

  4. 04

    Manufacture

    Glass cut or the sealed unit made to the measured size.

  5. 05

    Fit

    Fitted, glazed in, and the mess taken away.
