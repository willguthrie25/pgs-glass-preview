Commercial work is most of what we do. Fire-rated screens and doors that have to satisfy building control, shopfronts, high level and oversized glazing, and the replacement work that keeps a building running once it is occupied.

High level access is the part people tend to ask about. We use powered access and glass manipulators rather than improvising, and the glaziers doing it hold PASMA and IPAF qualifications.

If you are specifying, send the drawing. We would rather read it before quoting than price a guess and revise it later.
